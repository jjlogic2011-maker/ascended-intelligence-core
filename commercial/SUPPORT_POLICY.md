# Support Policy (Kernel Support SKU)

## Response targets (business hours PT unless upgraded)

| Severity | Target first response |
|----------|----------------------|
| Security vulnerability | 1 business day |
| Kernel regression blocking pilot | 2 business days |
| General guidance | 5 business days |

## Included

- Guidance on supported tags (N and N-1)  
- Security advisories  
- Clarification of LIMITATIONS  

## Not included

- Guaranteed uptime of Customer deployment  
- Custom feature development (separate SOW)  
- Compliance certifications
